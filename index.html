Run as Mobile App (iOS & Android)

No App Store needed • Works offline • Full-screen experience

🍎 For iPhone & iPad (Safari)
  1. Open this HTML page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (square box with an arrow pointing up at the bottom).
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
  4. Tap Add in the top-right corner.

✨ Why it's great: It launches in full-screen with no browser address bars, keeps your screen awake, and prevents iOS from forcing fullscreen video.

🤖 For Android (Chrome or Samsung Internet)
  1. Open this HTML page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three dots menu (⋮) in the top-right corner.
  3. Tap "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
  4. Tap Install to confirm.

✨ Why it's great: Full Web Speech API voice transcription, background media playback, and high performance.

💡 Tip for Local Files: When you tap Gallery/Photos, you can pick any recorded clip or voice memo directly from your camera roll or voice recorder.

Free AI Configuration

100% Free AI APIs & In-Browser Speech Engines

Built-in Free Browser AI (Zero Setup)

The app includes native Web Speech API (Chrome, Edge, Safari) and a client-side NLP smart summarizer that run 100% free offline with no keys required.

Groq Cloud AI (Optional Free Tier) Whisper & LLaMA-3

Groq provides an extremely fast, 100% free tier for Whisper Large v3 speech-to-text and LLaMA-3 translation & summarization.

Get your free key with zero card required at console.groq.com/keys. Stored only in your local browser storage.

Free Translation Endpoint

Uses the free public MyMemory / Lingva translation endpoints automatically. Translates transcripts segment-by-segment while keeping video timestamp synchronization intact.

Transcribing Instagram, Facebook & Movie Links

Browsers have strict security policies (CORS) that prevent client-side HTML apps from downloading raw video streams directly from sites like Instagram, Facebook, and DRM-protected movie sites without backend server credentials.

How to easily transcribe these in this app:

  1. 1-Click Save & Drop: Download the clip using free tools (like cobalt.tools or SaveFrom), then simply drop the file into the "Files / Gallery" tab!
  2. Direct Dictation: Switch to the "Direct Dictation" tab, hit Record, and play the video in another window or on your speaker. It will transcribe in real-time!
  3. YouTube & Podcasts: Paste direct YouTube links or podcast MP3 URLs directly in the URL tab. They work natively!
